Small questionshonest answers

?what if no cat chooses me?

then nothing is wrong with you. some people take ten visits. some take one. the croissants are good either way, and the cats remember faces.

?can i pick the cat up to see if we click?

no. nobody picks up a cat here, not even the staff. you sit, they come. that’s the whole system.

?do the three visits have to be in a row?

no rush. a week, a month. the cat will still be the cat. we just ask you to come back to the same one.

?what happens after the cat decides?

a vet check, a small home visit from us, a bag of their food and their favourite bake for the first morning. then they go home, and we put their photo on the went home wall.

?is it free?

adoption is free. we ask for a donation to the shelter we work with, any amount, and that you keep buying croissants from time to time. they’ll want to know.

come for a croissant.come back for them.
