We pressed pause

a bakery in taipei where the cats on the chairs are waiting for a home, and nobody is in a hurry. not the bread, not you, and definitely not the cats.

How itstarted

four moments, one cat, and a bakery that got too quiet.

  1. 06 · 2023

    It rained for nine days.

    a kitten sat outside our door every morning, same spot. someone left a clear umbrella over her. we put out a saucer of milk (wrong), then real food (right).

  2. 07 · 2023

    She came in. She didn’t leave.

    one morning the door was open for the flour delivery. she walked in, sat by the oven and fell asleep. we called her bubu. we didn’t decide that. she did.

  3. 01 · 2024

    Someone stayed three hours.

    a regular watched her sleep through one coffee, then a second coffee, then lunch. he came back the next day. and the next. bubu started saving him the chair.

  4. 04 · 2024

    She went home. We called the shelter.

    on 2 april bubu went home with him. by noon the bakery was too quiet. by friday we had a deal with the shelter on our street. by may, six cats on six chairs.

pawse

/pɔːz/ · 暂停 zàn tíng

  1. verb

    to stop for a moment, on purpose. the button on every remote. 暂停

  2. noun

    a paw, placed on your knee, that means “you are not leaving yet.”

  3. noun

    what a cat does in the middle of a room, for no reason, for about forty seconds.

  4. place

    a bakery on yongkang street. open 7:00 to 19:00. six chairs are taken.

“we’ll pawse here for a croissant.” “we pawsed for three hours.”
